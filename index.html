About

Videos come from Wikimedia Commons (public domain / Creative Commons, credited on every card) and from YouTube embeds of educational channels. "Did you know" cards are excerpts from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0, linked on each card). Tip cards are short written summaries of common guidance — not professional, medical, legal or financial advice. Everything you like, save and download stays on this device. No accounts, no tracking, no follower counts.
